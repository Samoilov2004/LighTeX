\section{Discussion}

The measured period increases with pendulum length, as predicted by
\autoref{eq:pendulum-period}. The largest sources of uncertainty are human
reaction time, the length measurement, and departures from the small-angle
approximation. Timing several oscillations reduces the relative effect of
starting and stopping the stopwatch.

\section{Conclusion}

The pendulum measurements give an illustrative estimate of
$g=\qty{9.81}{\meter\per\second\squared}$. A complete conclusion should state
whether the objective was met, quote the main result with uncertainty, and
identify the most important limitation without introducing new evidence.
