For $L=\qty{1.00}{\meter}$ and $T=\qty{2.006}{\second}$, the estimate is
\[
  g = \frac{4\pi^2(\qty{1.00}{\meter})}{(\qty{2.006}{\second})^2}
    = \qty{9.81}{\meter\per\second\squared}.
\]

Report measured values before interpreting them. Keep units in headings and
round calculated values to a precision justified by the measurements.
