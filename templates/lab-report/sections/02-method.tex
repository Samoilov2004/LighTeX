\section{Apparatus and method}

The setup consists of a support, a light string, a small mass, a meter rule,
and a stopwatch.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Measure the pendulum length from the pivot to the center of the mass.
  \item Displace the pendulum by a small angle and release it without pushing.
  \item Measure the time required for ten complete oscillations.
  \item Repeat the timing three times and calculate the mean period.
  \item Use \autoref{eq:gravity-estimate} to estimate $g$.
\end{enumerate}

Record enough procedural detail that another person could repeat the
experiment under comparable conditions.
