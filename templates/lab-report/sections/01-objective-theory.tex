\section{Objective and theory}

The objective is to estimate gravitational acceleration from the period of a
simple pendulum. For small angular displacements, the model predicts
\begin{equation}
  T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}},
  \label{eq:pendulum-period}
\end{equation}
where $T$ is the period, $L$ is the pendulum length, and $g$ is gravitational
acceleration. Solving \autoref{eq:pendulum-period} for $g$ gives
\begin{equation}
  g = \frac{4\pi^2L}{T^2}.
  \label{eq:gravity-estimate}
\end{equation}
