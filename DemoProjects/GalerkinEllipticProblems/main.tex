\begin{abstract$\Gamma$}
We study conforming finite-element approximations of a uniformly elliptic
Dirichlet problem. Starting from the variational formulation, we derive
Galerkin orthogonality, prove quasi-optimality in the energy norm, and obtain
an improved $L^2$ estimate by a duality argument. A manufactured solution on
the unit square illustrates the predicted convergence rates and separates the
roles of stability, approximation, and elliptic regularity in the analysis.
\end{abstract}
