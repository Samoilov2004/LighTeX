\section{Manufactured-solution experiment}
\label{sec:numerics}

Consider the Poisson equation on $\Omega=(0,1)^2$ with exact solution
\begin{equation}
  u(x,y)=\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y),
  \qquad
  f(x,y)=2\pi^2\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y).
  \label{eq:manufactured-solution}
\end{equation}
We use uniform diagonal triangulations and continuous $\mathbb P_1$ elements.
The observed rate between two consecutive meshes is
\begin{equation}
  \operatorname{rate}_\ell
  =\frac{\log(E_{\ell-1}/E_\ell)}{\log(h_{\ell-1}/h_\ell)}.
  \label{eq:observed-rate}
\end{equation}
